📘 How to read this map

Your mind is plotted as a point (𝒫, ℬ, 𝒯) in a 3D computational space. Mental state = Ψ(t) = F(𝒫, ℬ, 𝒯) + ξ(t); severity = distance from the origin ‖x‖ = √(𝒫²+ℬ²+𝒯²).

  • 𝒫 Precision (dopamine / salience): how strongly the brain weights incoming signals. High → everything feels cosmic & urgent. Low → flat, meaningless.
  • ℬ Boundary (serotonin / DMN): self–world demarcation. Porous (−) → merging, flooding. Rigid (+) → sealed off.
  • 𝒯 Temporal (horizon): past-locked (−) = replaying; future-locked (+) = wired ahead; integrated = connected timeline.
  • Bipolar reference coordinates: Mania (2, −1, 3) · Bipolar depression (−2, 1, −1.5) · Stable (0,0,0) · Mixed ≈ (−1, −0.5, 1.5).
  • Attractors pull trajectories in — the farther inside a basin, the harder the climb out (hysteresis). Watching your trajectory drift toward one early is the whole point of tracking.
  • This is a self-tracking metaphor, not a diagnostic instrument.

🌉 The BRIDGE Protocol

Memories are stored with state tags. Mania/pre-mania and medicated stability save memories under different internal keys — that's why manic memories can feel sealed off, unreal, or "not mine" when stable. You don't break the lock. You build a bridge between the two shores, with anchors.

Golden rules: never use sleep loss, stimulants, novelty or risk-taking to reach mania — that is re-triggering, not shadow work. Meds are the scaffold, not the silencer. Work below arousal 4/10. Stop any time. Not medical advice.

🌡️ Window of tolerance — right now

🛑 Arousal ≥ 4 — memory work is paused. Ground first: Anchor Kit → breathing, 5-4-3-2-1, ice, feet on floor. Tell someone if it keeps rising. Move the slider down when you're truly back in the window.
✅ Within window — you may do gentle bridge work. Touch memories for 10–20 s, then return to your anchor.

⏳ Temporal Collision Present-Anchoring — Technique 8

🌙 Sleep-Circadian Memory Anchor (Technique 12)

Sleep loss both triggers mania and re-encodes memory toward it. Sleep is the intervention, not just hygiene: fixed wake time · morning light · no stimulants after noon · wind-down ritual · bed as anchor. If sleep drops 2+ h or you feel wired → contact your team.

⛔ Abort protocol (memorize)

Stop the work → Ground (feet on floor, ice in hand, slow breathing) → Tell someone (staff / therapist / safe person) → Take meds exactly as prescribed → No stimulants → Sleep → Crisis line if it escalates.

🌅 Daily rhythm (social-rhythm therapy)

  • Same wake & sleep times — every day, weekends included
  • Morning light within 1 h of waking · dim evenings
  • Meds at the same time daily (use a pill box / alarm)
  • Meals, exercise, social contact at regular times
  • Daily: mood log + sleep log (this app's Today tab)
  • 2–3×/week: 20 min bridge journaling or parts dialogue
  • Monthly: review timeline & early warning signs
